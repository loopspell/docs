Loopspell integrates with Disqus to add a fully-featured comment thread to every post. When enabled, readers can log in with their Disqus, social, or guest accounts and leave comments directly on your posts.

## Setting Up a Disqus Account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Disqus">
    Visit [disqus.com](https://disqus.com) and sign up for a new account or log in to an existing one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your site">
    Click **Get Started** and choose **I want to install Disqus on my site**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Note your shortname">
    Enter your site name during setup. Disqus automatically generates a **shortname** for your site (for example, `myblog-12345`). Copy this shortname — you'll need it in the next step. You can also find it later under **Settings → General** in your Disqus admin panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a plan">
    Select the free **Basic** plan to complete setup.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Enabling Comments

Open your `_config.yml` file and add your shortname to the `disqus` key:

```yaml _config.yml
disqus: your-disqus-shortname
```

Replace `your-disqus-shortname` with the shortname Disqus assigned to your site — **not** your Disqus login username. Save the file to trigger a rebuild.

## How Comments Appear

After the rebuild completes, a Disqus comment thread appears automatically below every post on your blog. Readers see a comment input box along with options to sign in with a Disqus account, a social account, or post as a guest. No changes to your post templates are needed.

## Moderating Comments

Manage all reader comments from the Disqus dashboard at [disqus.com/admin](https://disqus.com/admin). From there you can:

- **Approve** comments held in the moderation queue
- **Flag or delete** spam or inappropriate content
- **Reply** to readers directly within the thread
- Configure moderation rules and email notifications under **Settings → Moderation**

## Disabling Comments

To turn off comments across your entire blog, remove the value from the `disqus` key in `_config.yml` or leave it blank:

```yaml _config.yml
disqus:
```

Save the file. After the next rebuild, the comment threads disappear from all posts.

<Warning>
  Disqus displays third-party advertisements on sites using the free Basic plan. If you prefer an ad-free experience for your readers, upgrade to a paid Disqus plan from your account settings at disqus.com.
</Warning>
