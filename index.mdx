## How Loopspell Works

Loopspell is built on [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com/) and hosted for free on [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/). You own your content, your repository, and your domain. There's nothing to deploy, no servers to manage, and no monthly hosting bill.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fork the Repository">
    Fork Loopspell to your GitHub account and rename it to `yourusername.github.io`. GitHub Pages will automatically build and serve your site at that URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Your Site">
    Open `_config.yml` in your browser and fill in your name, description, avatar URL, and any social links you want in the footer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write Your First Post">
    Edit the starter post in `/_posts/` or create a new `.md` file. GitHub Pages rebuilds your site within seconds every time you save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share Your Blog">
    Your blog is live at `https://yourusername.github.io`. Optionally connect a custom domain like `yourblog.com`.
  </Step>
</Steps>
