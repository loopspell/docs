You'll have a live, publicly accessible blog in under five minutes. There's no software to install, no accounts to create beyond a free GitHub account, and no commands to run. Everything happens right inside your browser.

<Warning>
  You must name your repository exactly `yourusername.github.io` — replacing `yourusername` with your actual GitHub username — for GitHub Pages to serve it as your user site. Any other name will not work for this URL format.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fork the Repository">
    Go to the [Loopspell repository](https://github.com/loopspell/loopspell.github.io) on GitHub and click **Fork** in the top-right corner. On the fork screen, rename the repository to `yourusername.github.io`, where `yourusername` is your exact GitHub username, then click **Create fork**.

    <Note>
      Your blog often goes live immediately after forking. GitHub recommends waiting up to 10 minutes for the very first build to complete if the site doesn't appear right away.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Customize Your Site">
    Open `_config.yml` in your new repository by clicking on the file name, then click the **pencil icon** to open the browser editor. Update at least the `name`, `description`, `avatar`, and `url` fields to make the blog your own:

    ```yaml _config.yml
    name: Your Blog Name
    description: A short bio or tagline
    avatar: https://yourdomain.com/your-avatar.jpg
    url: https://yourusername.github.io
    ```

    Click **Commit changes** when you're done. Any save to any file in the repository triggers a rebuild — your updated site will be live within seconds.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Visit Your Blog">
    Open your browser and navigate to `https://yourusername.github.io`. Your blog is live and already includes the Hello World starter post. Share the URL and start writing!
  </Step>
</Steps>
